Arturia MiniFreak V v4.0.2.6369
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Install the original setup:

	- MiniFreak_V__4_0_2_6369.exe

Once installed, 
copy and replace the .dll and .vst3 from the "Patch" folder in their corresponding directories.

VST3: C:\Program Files\Common Files\VST3

DLL: In your plugins folder

Besides you have to copy and replace these files in:

	C:\ProgramData\Arturia\MiniFreak V\x64
